# Candidate Test Guidance:

## Overview
This take-home test evaluates a candidate’s modeling skills through one main exercise:

- Theoretical model framework building from requirements

Candidates are allowed to use AI tools to support their work. However, during the interview, you should be prepared to explain the concepts, modeling choices, and solution approach you used in your submission.

## Submission Instructions ✅
Please submit your work by email to HR incharge.

## Scoring 🧮
 
- Theoretical and simulation network design exercise: 70 points
- Presentation and critical thinking: 30 points
Be prepared to present your methodology during the interview. There is no need for a fancy PPT — just organize your thoughts in a spreadsheet or any format of your choice.
- Bonus: Up to 20 extra points for Extensions Question in Interview
